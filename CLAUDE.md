## The game
The player is a half-octopus: their avatar rides in the middle of a big procedural octopus. Loop:
1. **Hub** (an island, walled W/N/E): stand on a training zone's floor and the octopus keeps lashing its dummy for XP (click / E / HIT hits faster). Levels give damage, HP, size and arms (4 -> 12); the level is capped per rebirth and Rebirth resets to Lv 1 for a permanent XP boost.
2. **Tentacle shop**: pedestals on the west side (+ two specials by the lane mouth). Each skin sets XP per training hit.
3. **Lane** (north of the hub): 15 stages, each a wave of bandits behind a pink LOCKED gate. Clear the wave -> the gate opens and two pads light (Wins / XP). Stepping on a pad cashes it and sends you home; walking on starts the next, exponentially harder stage. Stages 5/10/15 add a boss. Dying offers Revive / Revenge (paid in Wins) or No.
4. **Hub boss** (name is historical): a countdown on the HUD; at zero a giant bandit lands in the **lane**, ahead of the player, between waves (it waits while the timer is at 0 until the player is in the lane; leaving the lane removes it and it drops again next visit). Never in the hub. There is no skip: the timer just runs.
- Combat: the octopus auto-slaps the nearest bandit in reach; E / THROW grabs the bandits around it, lifts them and slams them down (cooldown). Bandits telegraph attacks on the ground (rect swing, line shot, circle smash) and only hit what is inside when it fills.
- **Meta progression** (HUD left menu, `progression.js` glues it): **Daily** (7-day streak calendar, a missed UTC day restarts it), **Gifts** (10 playtime gifts at 1..45 min, the clock carries over and restarts once all are claimed), **Inventory** = the "My ..." window with Skins / Items / Titles / Trails tabs, **Rebirth** (popup; Skip Rebirth = keep levels, paid in Wins), **Ascend** (spend ALL Wins for a tier: x damage, bigger + faster; Skip Ascend = pay more, keep the rest). Items are boosts you hold (2x Train / 2x Wins / 2x Damage / Speed, 5 min each, timers stack, shown bottom right). 36 titles unlock from stats (goal per title in `TITLES`); the equipped one floats over the octopus and gives its % bonus. Trails (Wins) stream behind the octopus. Signed-in players' Daily/Gifts are server-authoritative (claims go to the server, the reward is granted on its ok); guests/offline run the same rules locally (`DAILY.storageKey`).
- **Every price is in Wins** (see the root CLAUDE.md: never premium currency).

## Stack
Three.js (vanilla) + Vite + Rapier (`@dimforge/rapier3d-compat`, wasm inlined, top-level await -> `build.target: 'es2022'`). Everything is procedural (canvas textures, synthesized audio); the only imported model is the Bloxity base rig `public/avatars/player.glb`. Font: Fredoka.
- `npm run dev` / `npm run build`. `VITE_DEV_MODE=true` skips the Bloxity SDK/CDN. Preview config `octopus` in `.claude/launch.json` (port 5231, dev mode).
- All tunables are in `src/config.js`: identity, `HUB` / `LANE` layout (+ `stageStartZ`/`stageEndZ`/`gateZ`/`padZ`), `PROGRESSION` (XP curve, level cap, arms table, damage/HP growth), `TENTACLES`, `ZONES` + `ZONE_LAYOUT`, `SHOP_LAYOUT`, `STAGES` (recommended levels, rewards, bandit scaling), `COMBAT`, `HUB_BOSS`, `TRAINING`, `BOOSTS` (Wins prices), `REBIRTH` (skip price), `ASCEND`, `ITEMS`, `DAILY`, `PLAYTIME`, `TITLES`, `TRAILS`, `AUDIO`, `JUICE`, plus the base look/camera/physics/rig sections.
- Dev console (dev builds): `__game.give(1000)`, `__game.levels(10)`, `__game.teleport(x, y, z)`, `__game.reset()`, `__game.state`.

## Mobile & responsive (required for every change)
The game must play on phones and tablets (portrait and landscape) as well as desktop. Every action needs a touch path (THROW button, HIT button while training, tappable prompts). Use Pointer Events, `matchMedia('(pointer: coarse)')` for touch UI, relative units, tap targets >= 44px, safe-area insets, `touch-action: none` on game controls, react to `resize`. Respect the graphics presets (DPR, shadow map, bloom off on phones). Check UI changes at 375x812 and 812x375 with touch emulation plus desktop.

## Bloxity platform rules (docs.bloxity.io, required to publish)
- Boxity draws the menu / pause, chat, emote wheel, avatar editor, invites / friends / parties, the leaderboard trophy button and sign in / out: the game never draws its own (no Log in / Gems-shop / leaderboard buttons). The hub boards are in-world pillars, not a button. Escape opens `portal.showMenu()`.
- Keep the HUD out of the reserved areas (`bloxity.onReservedAreas`). Never bind P.
- The player can die: `combat.die()` calls `bloxity.showDeathScreen()`; Revive / Revenge / No / Watch Ad respawn ONLY when `gate.respawn()` is true (never on the game's own timer while the screen is open); the dialog's own 6 s timer is the fallback outside Boxity. A rewarded ad (`showRewardedAd('revive')`) buys a free revive, granted only on `completed`.
- Ads: pause + mute between `onAdBreak` start / end (`main.js`); `ADS.screens` declared at init, `adScreen('gameplay' | 'panel')` as windows open / close.
- Emotes (`registerFeature('emotes')` in `bloxity.js` init): never hardcode ids, ignore unknown ones, stop when moving; the id is relayed to the others (`emote` message).
- Gems / IAP are deliberately not integrated (see the root CLAUDE.md: everything is priced in Wins).
- Not integrated (no fit): Unity bridge, hub-games `platform` API, `party.enable` (parties are automatic on Legion hosting), `play_with_friends` (own private rooms), custom characters' own GLB (the kit / fallback draws the regular avatar), hand / shoe accessories in the manual fallback (the kit draws them).
